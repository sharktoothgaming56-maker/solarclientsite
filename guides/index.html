How these guides are written

A note on method, because there is a great deal of confidently wrong Minecraft performance advice in circulation.

Every guide here tries to explain a mechanism rather than hand you a list of steps. Knowing that Sodium helps is less useful than knowing it helps because vanilla issues too many small draw calls — because the second version tells you when it will help a lot, when it will help little, and what to try instead.

Where we give numbers, we give ranges and say what they depend on. Minecraft performance varies enormously with hardware, render distance, world content, and where you happen to be standing, and any guide quoting a single exact multiplier is quoting one measurement on one machine. We would rather tell you how to measure it yourself, in a fixed spot, with the Alt+F3 frame time graph open.

Where SolarClient does something relevant, we say so — but every mod, flag, and technique described here works without it. The mods are open source and freely available, the JVM flags are public, and none of it is exclusive to any launcher. If the guides are useful and you never download anything from us, that is a perfectly good outcome.

Found something wrong, or have a question a guide does not answer? Get in touch — corrections are genuinely welcome, and the guides get updated when they are.
