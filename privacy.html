Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Summary: SolarClient does not collect personal data through this website. The launcher authenticates through Microsoft’s official OAuth flow and never sees your password. This site uses Google AdSense, which sets cookies for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time.

1. Who We Are

SolarClient is an independently developed Minecraft launcher. This privacy policy explains how the SolarClient website (this site) and the SolarClient desktop application (“the launcher”) handle information. When we say “we,” “us,” or “our,” we mean the SolarClient development team.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Website

This website does not collect personal information directly. We do not have sign-up forms, contact forms, or user accounts on the website. The site is hosted on our secure hosting provider, which may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, browser type, timestamps) as part of its hosting infrastructure.

2.2 Launcher Application

The SolarClient launcher collects the following information locally on your device:

  • Microsoft account session token: Used to authenticate your Minecraft account through Microsoft’s official OAuth 2.0 flow. The token is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to SolarClient servers. We never see or store your Microsoft password.
  • Launcher preferences: Settings such as allocated RAM, selected Minecraft version, mod selections, HUD layout, and waypoints are stored locally in the launcher’s configuration directory on your computer.
  • Crash logs: If the launcher crashes, a log file is created locally. We may ask you to share crash logs voluntarily through Discord for debugging purposes, but they are never collected automatically.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or physical address.
  • We do not collect gameplay data, chat messages, or server activity.
  • We do not collect system hardware specifications or telemetry.
  • We do not track your usage of the launcher application.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdSense

This website displays advertisements through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. This may involve the collection of:

  • Cookie identifiers
  • IP address (anonymized by Google in the EEA)
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and interaction data on this site

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices. For more information, see How Google Uses Information from Sites That Use Our Services.

3.2 Microsoft OAuth

When you log in through the launcher, you are redirected to Microsoft’s official login page. SolarClient receives only an OAuth access token and a Minecraft profile (your in-game username and UUID). We do not receive or store your Microsoft password. Microsoft’s privacy policy governs the data Microsoft collects during the login process: Microsoft Privacy Statement.

3.3 File Hosting

When you download SolarClient, our hosting provider may log your IP address and request metadata to securely deliver the file. We do not track individual downloads.

3.4 Modrinth and CurseForge

The launcher’s mod browser fetches mod listings from the Modrinth and CurseForge APIs. These requests are made directly from your device to their servers. Please refer to Modrinth’s Privacy Policy and CurseForge/Overwolf’s Privacy Policy for information about data they may collect.

4. Cookies

This website uses cookies primarily through Google AdSense for advertising purposes. These are third-party cookies set by Google, not by SolarClient. We do not set any first-party tracking cookies. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or through Google’s opt-out mechanisms linked above. Our cookie policy sets out in detail what is stored, why, and every way to switch off personalised advertising. Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message through Google’s certified consent management platform before any personalised advertising cookies are set.

5. Data Storage and Security

All user data (settings, session tokens, waypoints) is stored locally on your device in the launcher’s configuration directory. No personal data is transmitted to or stored on SolarClient-operated servers. We do not operate backend servers that store user information.

6. Children’s Privacy

SolarClient does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The website does not have user accounts or registration forms. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us through Discord or another channel, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share personal data with third parties. The only third-party data processing that occurs is through the services listed in Section 3, which operate independently under their own privacy policies.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you (in practice, we hold none)
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising (via Google Ads Settings)
  • Control cookies through your browser settings

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, most data-related requests should be directed to the relevant third-party service (Google, Microsoft, GitHub).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the website or launcher after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, the best way to reach us is through our Discord server:
