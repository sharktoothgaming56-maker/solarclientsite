Performance Tips

  • Allocate 3–4 GB of RAM for the best balance of performance and stability. More isn’t always better—too much RAM can increase garbage collection pauses.
  • SolarClient’s built-in Sodium renderer replaces OptiFine. They can’t run together, but Sodium typically delivers higher FPS.
  • Use the built-in mod browser to find shader packs compatible with Iris, the Sodium-friendly shader loader.
  • Lower your render distance to 8–12 chunks in PvP modes for smoother gameplay during intense fights.
  • The launcher auto-applies optimized JVM flags. You don’t need to paste custom arguments unless you know what you’re doing.
